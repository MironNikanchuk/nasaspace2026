# MoonSight — 30-second demo voiceover

Read at a natural pace. Leave a short beat after the opening question. Let the screen show the terrain toggle while the narrator says “terrain.”

| Time | On screen | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00–06 | MoonSight homepage and Moon | Curious, a brief pause after the question |
| 06–14 | Two landing sites in Compare | Clear and steady |
| 14–23 | Switch from geometric horizon to LOLA terrain | Emphasize the visibility change |
| 23–30 | Timeline, team and GitHub | Confident, short pause before the final phrase |

**English**

Where on the Moon can a lander see both the Sun and Earth? MoonSight lets you compare landing sites near the lunar south pole. At Connecting Ridge, adding terrain drops Earth visibility from forty-one to twenty-eight percent for this week. Move through time to see when both are visible. We’re GameOver. The code and data are on GitHub.

Код и данные на GitHub.
